Approach

How I think about software quality.

  1. Start with risk, not ceremony. Testing should clarify the real failure modes before it adds process.
  2. Favor maintainable systems. Good quality work improves the product and the codebase, not just the test report.
  3. Keep feedback loops practical. Fast, trustworthy checks matter more than elaborate but fragile pipelines.
  4. Communicate clearly. Quality becomes useful when findings, trade-offs, and release decisions are easy to understand.
